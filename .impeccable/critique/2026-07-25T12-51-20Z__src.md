## Calorie MVP critique

### Heuristic summary

The initial implementation scored 26/40. Its strongest qualities were a warm,
distinctive voice, an effective one-handed mobile layout, and a sound
accessibility foundation. The visual system felt friendly and the primary
navigation was easy to scan.

### Anti-pattern verdict

No generic dashboard-template, gradient-heavy, or AI-product visual tropes were
present. The main risks were interaction rigidity and a few semantic gaps rather
than a weak aesthetic direction.

### Priority issues

- **[P1] Food logging was too rigid and irreversible.** One-tap defaults were
  useful, but there was no clear way to correct amount/time mistakes or undo a
  deletion. Add a proper entry composer, edit/delete actions, and undo.
- **[P1] Eating gaps read as an achievement.** The fasting metric risked
  moralizing a neutral behavioral pattern. Use neutral eating-gap language and
  keep thresholds user-controlled.
- **[P1] Progress relied too heavily on colour.** Add markers and a textual
  summary so trends remain understandable without colour discrimination.
- **[P2] Several touch targets and type roles were undersized.** Bring controls
  to at least 44px and consolidate typography into the documented rem scale.
- **[P2] Food deletion and validation feedback needed safer anchoring.** Add
  inline field errors and an explicit deletion confirmation.

### Persona red flags

**Frequent logger:** A mistaken one-tap food could not be corrected without
friction, undermining trust in the daily totals.

**First-time tracker:** The original fasting presentation could be interpreted
as a score to maximize, while colour-only charts made progress less legible.

**Mobile accessibility user:** A handful of compact controls were below the
recommended touch size, and explanatory methodology copy was too small.

### Minor observations

The cream/sage card treatment was slightly more generic than the botanical
direction. Type sizes and font weights had accumulated too many near-duplicate
values.

### Questions

- Can the main journal remain useful even when a user never creates an account?
- Does every health estimate explain its assumption without sounding
  prescriptive?
- Can every quick action be corrected immediately?
