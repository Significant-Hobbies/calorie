# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is someone logging food with one hand, often immediately
after eating, and checking progress in short bursts throughout the day. The
first user is the owner. The default journal stays in browser storage with no
account; a Google-authenticated D1 journal is an optional cloud-backed mode.

## Product Purpose

Calorie makes lightweight nutrition tracking useful beyond a calorie total. It
records food, four core nutrients, water, medication routines, weight, and
personal goals, then turns those
inputs into transparent daily targets and practical timing estimates for
training, fasting, and sleep. Success means logging a familiar meal takes
seconds and every recommendation can be traced to a visible formula or rule.

## Positioning

A small food journal that turns four macros and your goal into useful timing,
without an opaque coach or AI layer.

## Brand Personality

Cute, clear, and reassuring. Calorie feels like a bright botanical pocket
journal: encouraging without celebrating restriction, precise without
clinical severity, and friendly without childish language.

## Anti-references

No punitive diet dashboards, anxiety-inducing red deficit meters, bodybuilding
control panels, gamified streak pressure, or generic pastel wellness
glassmorphism. The interface must not imply medical certainty.

## Design Principles

1. Logging comes first: the primary action stays reachable and familiar.
2. Show the working: recommendations include the input and rule that produced
   them.
3. Progress, not punishment: goals use maintenance-relative ranges and trends instead of moral
   judgments about food.
4. Ask only what the math needs, explain sensitive inputs, and let users edit
   or omit them.
5. Be delightful in tone and restraint, never at the expense of legibility or
   speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The mobile web app targets WCAG 2.2 AA, supports 200% zoom, 44px touch targets,
visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and status cues that do not rely on
color alone. Gender identity is optional. The energy estimate separately asks
which published equation profile to use and offers a no-estimate path.
